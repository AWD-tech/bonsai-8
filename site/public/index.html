Connect your SP–1

Your player, on screen.

Connect to follow the player’s controls, lights and playback position. Choose songs for either deck under On your player.

  1. 01
    Connect a USB data cable.Finish any transfer and close other apps connected to the player.
  2. 02
    Power on normally.Keep USB connected and hold FUNCTION for 1.5 seconds if the player is off. Normal playback does not use bootloader mode.
  3. 03
    Connect your player below.Select Bonsai 8 (or SP–1 Dual Deck on earlier firmware). If USB is unavailable in this browser, open the site in Chrome or Edge.

After connecting, choose Listen here to hear the player through your computer and see its waveform. USB audio adds a small delay. Connection help

About Bonsai 8

Two songs.
Your own mix.

Bonsai 8 is custom firmware and a web companion for the SP–1. Mix stems from two songs on your player, then use this site to prepare music, manage your library and follow the mix.

You can also separate and mix a song in the browser without a player. Your music is processed on your own device.

Bonsai 8 firmware

Make it Bonsai 8.

Checking the published release…

  1. 01
    Disconnect the site.Close other player connections. Your uploaded songs stay on the player.
  2. 02
    Enter bootloader mode.On the powered-on SP–1, hold Track 1 + Track 4 for three seconds. Leave USB connected.
  3. 03
    Choose the bootloader below.Keep USB connected until the update finishes. Then hold FUNCTION to power on normally.

Only the application is updated. The updater verifies its responses; it cannot read back the installed application. Live playback still needs checking after installation.
