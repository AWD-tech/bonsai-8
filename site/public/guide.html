01

Find your way around.

SongA loaded piece of music, split into up to four stems.

StemOne part: vocals, drums, bass or other instruments.

Deck A / Deck BTwo independent songs that can play together.

Selected deckThe deck your physical faders and buttons currently control.

02

Start with one song.

  1. Hold FUNCTION for about 1.5 seconds to power on. Deck A is selected and paused.
  2. Start with the master low. Move each fader to its top end, then lower it to the level you want.
  3. Tap and release PLAY. Tap again to pause the selected deck.

A tap is shorter than half a second. Holding PLAY for about half a second returns that song to the beginning, paused. Hold FUNCTION alone for 1.5 seconds to power off.

03

Any song, on either deck.

  1. Tap FUNCTION to choose A or B. Its outside status light tells you which deck is selected.
  2. Hold FUNCTION, then tap the rocker to move to the previous or next loaded song. Repeat until you find one.
  3. Release both controls. The new song is at its beginning, paused. Tap PLAY to bring it in.

Change B while A plays, or A while B plays. Loading a song keeps that deck’s existing levels, mutes, speed and effects.

04

Vocals from B. Instruments from A.

  1. On A, choose your instrumental song. Mute stem 1 with its button. Set faders 2–4, then tap PLAY.
  2. Tap FUNCTION to select B. Choose your vocal song. Move all four faders to minimum, then raise only fader 1 and make sure it is unmuted. Keep stems 2–4 muted.
  3. Tap PLAY when you want the vocal to enter. Balance it with fader 1. Switch back to A to adjust the instruments.

Check mutes before playing: a stem button toggles its state. Songs do not automatically match beats or musical keys. Start the second song by ear.

Pause one deck

Select it, then tap PLAY.

Pause both

While both play, hold FUNCTION and tap PLAY.

05

Balance it. Bend the pitch.

Faders control one stem each. When you switch decks, move a fader through its saved position before it takes over. A blinking track light means pickup is waiting.

Tap a stem button to mute. Hold it for about half a second to solo that stem on the selected deck; release to restore the mix.

The rocker changes speed and pitch together on the selected deck, from 0.5× to 1.25× speed. To bend only the vocal, isolate it on B first.

Pitch affects the whole selected deck, not an individual stem. There is no automatic beat/key sync or pitch-preserving stretch. Volume + / − controls the master for both decks.

06

Give a stem some character.

  1. Select your deck. Hold FUNCTION, then the stem button you want to shape.
  2. Keep both held. Tap the rocker to choose filter, echo or reverb. Side light 1, 2 or 3 shows the choice.
  3. Move that stem’s fader through its saved effect amount, then adjust. After a fresh power-on, begin at minimum to pick up its zero amount.
  4. Release. The effect stays on; the fader returns to volume with pickup.

One effect per stem. Changing its type keeps the amount. Set the amount to zero to bypass. Echo is a fixed 250 ms repeat; echo and reverb have an intentionally dark sound. Effects are not beat-synced and reset when you power off.

07

Keep the mix.

  1. Start your mix. Hold FUNCTION and both volume buttons for one second, then release. Slowly blinking side lights mean recording.
  2. Perform your mix. Use the same combination again to stop.
  3. Pause both decks. If both are playing, FUNCTION + PLAY pauses both. Otherwise select and pause the one still playing.
  4. Keep the player powered while it saves. Wait for the normal deck lights to return.

The take becomes a new song in the first empty slot. Its whole stereo mix is on stem 1. Connect later to export it from the site.

You need an empty slot. Maximum take: about eight minutes. Fast blinking means draining or waiting to save; blinking outer lights indicate failure. Song changes are blocked until recording and saving finish.

08

Your player. A bigger view.

  1. On a computer, open the Bonsai 8 site in a browser with Web Serial support, such as desktop Chrome or Edge.
  2. Power on the player normally and connect a USB data cable. Choose Connect SP-1, then the Bonsai 8 / Dual Deck mode and your device.
  3. Play on the physical player. Watch the mirrored controls, lights and deck timers. Choose Listen here to monitor its USB mix when a compatible player audio input is available.

Manage songs

Use On your player to load either deck, export a song or delete its slot. Prepare stems on the site, then upload into an empty slot.

Install firmware

Choose Install Bonsai 8 in the site footer. Follow the updater’s bootloader steps. Wait for completion before unplugging.

The connected model mirrors the hardware; its local mixer does not remotely control physical levels. Uploads and transfers pause playback. Browsers without Web Serial, including most mobile browsers, can read this guide but cannot connect or flash the player.
